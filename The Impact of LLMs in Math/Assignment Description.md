# Assignment Description

**Due date: September 30**

The first assignment includes four components:

## 1. Your “LLM”: a collection of at least 12 sources

Build an information repository on your chosen topic with **12 or more sources**. Obsidian and NotebookLM will be demonstrated in class as tools for this work.

## 2. Source log

Provide a list of the sources in your collection. For each source, identify what it is, its authorship, and a brief description of its content. Include identifying information such as its title and a link or location so it can be found again.

## 3. Calibration test

Query your LLM and consider its response. Write a few sentences evaluating the output—for example, whether it accurately represents your sources, leaves out relevant information, or makes unsupported claims.

## 4. Reflective essay (“familiar essay”)

Describe the **what, how, and why** of your LLM:

- **What:** What your repository is and what it includes.
- **How:** How you collected your sources and decided what to include or leave out.
- **Why:** Why you chose your topic or approach, and why the collection works for you as a representative information repository on that topic.

The essay should reflect your own interests, decisions, and experience building the collection.
